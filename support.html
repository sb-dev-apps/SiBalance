Common Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. SiBalance does not require user accounts.

Does SiBalance connect to my bank?

No. SiBalance does not connect to bank accounts or financial institutions.

Where is my data stored?

Your budgeting data is stored locally on your device. Optional iCloud sync is available for supported premium users.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open SiBalance and select Restore Purchases from the Premium screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through your Apple ID subscription settings in the App Store.

Does SiBalance use ads or analytics?

No. SiBalance does not use advertising services, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or marketing SDKs.
