Getting Started

Create or Restore Your First Budget

When you first open SiBalance, choose how you want to begin.

  • New Budget: start again with a new budget
  • Check iCloud Backup: look for existing SiBalance data in iCloud
  • Restore Encrypted Backup: restore from a manual .sibackup file
  • Restore Purchases: restore an existing SiBalance Lifetime purchase

On smaller phones, the setup screen can scroll so all setup options remain reachable.

You can restore an existing Lifetime purchase if a feature requires Lifetime access.

Choose Your Currency

  • Browse the supported currencies
  • Search by currency code or name
  • Tap Continue to confirm your selection

Optional iCloud Sync

You can choose whether to enable iCloud Sync.

When enabled, budgets, transactions, categories, and settings are synchronised across your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account.

If you reinstall SiBalance or move to a new device, the app can automatically search for existing SiBalance data in iCloud and restore it during setup.

During setup, SiBalance shows clearer iCloud restore states such as checking, found, not found, unavailable, and restoring. If an iCloud backup is found but requires Lifetime access, the restore option will guide you through Lifetime purchase access first.

If you tap New Budget and SiBalance finds existing iCloud data, it will ask whether you want to restore that iCloud backup, start a new budget anyway, or cancel. If no iCloud data is found, New Budget continues normally.

If you deliberately start a new budget despite existing iCloud data, SiBalance will not immediately prompt you to turn on iCloud Sync. This helps avoid accidentally replacing an older iCloud backup.

Restoring an encrypted backup during first launch restores data locally on the device. It does not automatically enable iCloud Sync.

Select a Budget Period

Choose how your budget is organised:

  • Weekly: useful for close tracking and shorter planning cycles
  • Fortnightly: groups your budget every two weeks
  • Monthly: the most common budgeting period
  • Yearly: provides an annual budgeting view

You can also choose the day your budget period starts.

Income

Income entries represent money coming into your budget.

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter an amount
  3. Add an optional source name
  4. Choose a date
  5. Enable Repeat for recurring income

Examples include salary, pensions, benefits, freelance income, and other payments.

Expenses

Expense entries represent money leaving your budget.

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter an amount
  3. Add an optional merchant or description
  4. Choose a date
  5. Enable Repeat for recurring expenses

Examples include rent, utilities, insurance, transport, and other bills.

Deduct from Safe to Spend

When adding a one-off expense, use Deduct from Safe to Spend to decide whether it should reduce your Safe To Spend allowance.

Leave it switched on for everyday spending, such as groceries, shopping, meals out, fuel, or other costs you want to deduct from Safe To Spend.

Switch it off for planned one-off bills or committed costs, such as an annual insurance payment, car repair, school fee, or a bill that only happens once. The expense will still reduce your balance, but it will not use up your Safe To Spend ring.

If you turn on Repeat for an expense, SiBalance automatically treats it as committed spending. Repeating expenses still reduce your balance and daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly allowance, but they do not count as spending against the Safe To Spend ring.

Safe To Spend uses your selected window, such as daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, yearly, or the full budget period. Expenses with Deduct from Safe to Spend switched on only reduce the ring inside that selected window.

Searching, Editing and Deleting Entries

Searching Entries

Use the search button on the Income or Expenses screen to find entries by name, category, note, amount, date, or frequency.

Search starts within the period shown in the period bar. To look beyond the current period, tap Search all periods. This expands the results to matching entries across other periods too.

When searching all periods, recurring entries are shown as individual occurrences so you can find the exact payment or bill you need.

One-off entries appear only on their actual entry date. Recurring entries can appear from their original start date, so older matches are included for as far back as you have data for that item.

If a recurring entry has an end date, search includes occurrences from the start date through to that end date. If a recurring entry has no end date, Search all periods includes upcoming occurrences for up to one year ahead from your current budget period or today, whichever is later.

Use the sort control while searching to reorder results by newest, oldest, amount, or name. Changing the search scope does not change your selected sort order.

Editing Entries

Income and expense rows use swipe actions for editing. Swipe left on an income or expense row, then tap Edit.

For one-time entries, the edit screen opens directly.

For repeating entries, choose how much of the series to change:

  • Edit This Entry: changes only that single occurrence
  • Edit All Entries: changes the full repeating series

If one occurrence from a repeating series has been edited separately, the row shows an Edited pill next to Repeating. This shows that the entry is still connected to a repeating series, but has been individually changed.

Deleting Entries

Swipe left on an income or expense row, then tap Delete.

For one-time entries, confirm the delete action to remove that entry.

For recurring entries, choose how much of the series to remove:

  • Delete This Entry: removes only that single occurrence and leaves the rest of the recurring series in place
  • Delete This and Future Entries: removes the selected occurrence and all later occurrences, while keeping earlier entries
  • Delete All Entries: removes the entire recurring series

Deleting an item updates the current period totals, dashboard totals, insights, and widgets.

These choices help prevent accidentally changing or deleting an entire repeating income or bill when you only meant to update one occurrence.

Safe To Spend

Safe To Spend helps you understand how much money is available after your budget commitments are considered.

It is designed to answer a practical question: how much can you safely spend without putting this budget period under pressure?

Safe To Spend starts with your remaining budget balance, subtracts any active safety buffer, then spreads the available amount across the remaining time in the budget period.

Depending on your budget, the calculation may include:

  • Available balance
  • Future income
  • Future expenses
  • Recurring transactions
  • Safety buffer
  • Available to spend

Safe To Spend Period

You can choose how Safe To Spend is shown from Settings > Balance > Safe to spend. The setting is shown as Frequency because it controls how Safe To Spend is split over time.

Options include:

  • Daily
  • Weekly
  • Fortnightly
  • Monthly
  • Yearly
  • Match Period

For example, if Safe To Spend is set to Weekly, the Dashboard and widgets show the amount available for the current week. If it is set to Match Period, SiBalance shows the amount available for the rest of the current budget period.

Weekly and fortnightly Safe To Spend windows use the chosen week start when the budget itself is weekly or fortnightly. For other budget intervals, those windows use Monday starts.

Safe To Spend settings are saved per budget. One budget can use Weekly while another uses Daily, and SiBalance will remember the correct choice when you switch between budgets.

Safety Buffer

The safety buffer lets you reserve part of your money before Safe To Spend is calculated. This can help keep money aside for unexpected costs or simply give your budget more breathing room.

If a safety buffer is active, Safe To Spend is calculated after that amount has been reserved.

Widgets

Safe To Spend widgets reflect the Safe To Spend period chosen for each budget. If your budget is set to Weekly, the widget shows the weekly amount. If it is set to Monthly, the widget shows the monthly amount.

Smaller Lock Screen widgets may show a compact amount only, while larger widgets can show more context such as the selected period and budget status.

Notifications

SiBalance can send local reminders for upcoming budget events. Notifications are optional and can be changed at any time from Settings > Notifications.

Available reminders include:

  • Expense due: reminds you before saved expenses are due
  • Income due: reminds you before expected income dates
  • Period ending: reminds you before the current budget period ends

You can choose how far in advance due reminders appear. This applies to expense, income, and period ending reminders.

Notifications are created locally on your device. SiBalance does not send your budget data to a server to create reminders.

If notifications do not appear, check that notifications are enabled for SiBalance in iOS Settings.

Accessibility

SiBalance includes accessibility support across key budgeting flows.

  • VoiceOver support: key dashboard cards, Safe To Spend, income and expense actions, settings rows, purchase options, and controls include accessibility labels, values, and hints.
  • Reduced Motion support: SiBalance respects iOS Reduce Motion and includes an in-app Reduce Dashboard Motion setting.
  • Dynamic Type support: many screens support larger text sizes, wrapping, and layout adjustments.
  • High contrast and legibility support: backgrounds and decorative visuals adapt for increased contrast, and decorative imagery is hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Large touch targets: important controls use accessible hit areas and clear labels.
  • Balance privacy controls: balances can be hidden or revealed to reduce visual exposure.

Reduce Motion

If you prefer fewer animations and motion effects, enable Reduce Motion in iOS Settings. SiBalance automatically respects this setting.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap Accessibility
  3. Tap Motion
  4. Turn on Reduce Motion

Security & Storage

Protect the app, manage sync, and create or restore encrypted backups.

Device Passcode / Face ID

Enable Device Passcode or Face ID to require device authentication when opening SiBalance.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync keeps your budgets, transactions, categories, and settings available across your Apple devices.

When iCloud Sync is off, your data remains stored locally on your device.

Encrypted Backups

SiBalance can create manual encrypted backup files using the .sibackup format. Go to Settings > Security & Storage > Advanced Privacy > Create Encrypted Backup.

Backup creation asks you to enter and confirm a password, and to acknowledge that the password cannot be recovered if forgotten. You can show or hide the password while creating the backup.

The default filename is SiBalance Backup YYYY-MM-DD.sibackup. Backups include budgets, income, expenses, transactions, categories, recurring items, and settings.

Backups are encrypted using AES-GCM with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256.

Restoring an Encrypted Backup

To restore a manual backup, go to Settings > Security & Storage > Advanced Privacy > Restore Encrypted Backup.

  1. Choose a .sibackup file
  2. Enter the backup password
  3. Confirm the restore

Restoring a backup replaces the current local SiBalance data on this device.

If iCloud Sync is enabled and you have Lifetime access, SiBalance can offer Restore and Update iCloud. This restores the backup locally and pushes the restored data to iCloud so your other devices can receive it.

If the iCloud update fails, SiBalance keeps the local restore and queues sync for later.

After restoring an encrypted backup, SiBalance checks your App Store purchase status before deciding what access message to show. This helps the app recognise existing Lifetime access correctly.

Restoring Data After Reinstalling SiBalance

If iCloud Sync was enabled before you removed SiBalance, the app will automatically check for existing SiBalance data in your iCloud account when you reinstall it.

If matching data is found, SiBalance can restore your budgets, transactions, categories, and settings from iCloud during setup.

This can help you move to a new device or reinstall the app without losing previously synced data.

Managing iCloud Data

Additional iCloud management options may be available when iCloud Sync has been used.

  • Stop Using iCloud: stops syncing and keeps this device's data stored locally in SiBalance.

Delete iCloud Data is available from the Data section. Use it carefully, as deleting iCloud data may affect other devices connected to the same iCloud account.

Lifetime Purchase

Manage or restore your SiBalance Lifetime purchase through Apple's purchase systems.

  • View Lifetime access
  • Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases if you already bought SiBalance Lifetime and need to restore access on this device. If Apple's sign-in or restore prompt is cancelled, SiBalance will show that the restore was cancelled instead of saying purchases were restored.

If a restore sync problem occurs but the App Store already shows valid Lifetime access, SiBalance can still restore access from the current App Store entitlement.

Data

Use Data settings for permanent actions.

Delete iCloud Data removes SiBalance data from iCloud while keeping this device local.

Delete All Data removes all saved budget data from this app. This action cannot be undone.
