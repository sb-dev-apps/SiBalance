SiBalance User Guide

SiBalance helps you plan your money clearly, track income and expenses, and understand how much you can safely spend throughout your budget period.

Privacy First

SiBalance is designed to keep your financial data private.

  • No account required
  • No bank connections
  • No tracking or advertising
  • No third-party analytics
  • Data stored on your device
  • Optional private iCloud Sync

Getting Started

Create Your First Budget

When you open SiBalance for the first time, tap New Budget.

You can start with a free trial of SiBalance Premium or restore an existing purchase.

Premium Features

  • Safe To Spend calculations
  • Recurring income and expenses
  • Unlimited transactions
  • Full transaction history
  • Global currencies
  • Insights and forecasting
  • Private iCloud Sync
  • Multiple budgets
  • Widgets and exports
  • Siri support

Choose Your Currency

Select the currency you want to use for your budget.

  • Browse the currency list
  • Search by currency code or name
  • Tap Continue to confirm your selection

Optional iCloud Sync

After choosing your currency, SiBalance may ask whether you want to turn on iCloud Sync.

iCloud Sync keeps your budgets, transactions, categories, and settings available across your Apple devices.

You can turn iCloud Sync on or off later in Settings.

Name Your Budget

Enter a name you will recognise at a glance.

Examples:

  • Monthly Budget
  • Family Budget
  • Household Budget
  • Personal Budget

The budget name appears on your Dashboard, Income, Expenses, and Insights screens.

Select Your Budget Period

Choose how SiBalance groups your money.

Weekly

Best for close tracking and shorter planning cycles.

Fortnightly

Groups your budget every two weeks.

Monthly

The most common budgeting period.

Yearly

Provides an annual budgeting view.

You can also choose when the budget period starts. This can be changed later in Settings.

Add Income

You can add income during setup or skip this step and add it later from the Income tab.

For each income entry:

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter the amount
  3. Add an optional income source
  4. Choose the date
  5. Turn on Repeat if the income happens regularly

Examples of income include salary, benefits, pension, freelance income, and other regular payments.

Add Expenses

You can add expenses during setup or skip this step and add them later from the Expenses tab.

For each expense:

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter the amount
  3. Add an optional merchant or name
  4. Choose the date
  5. Turn on Repeat if the expense happens regularly

Examples of expenses include rent, utilities, insurance, subscriptions, transport, and other bills.

Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a clear overview of your current budget.

It shows:

  • Current balance
  • Budget period
  • Total income
  • Total expenses
  • Safe To Spend amount

Use the left and right arrows to move between budget periods.

Hide or Reveal Balances

Tap the eye icon to hide or reveal your balances.

Safe To Spend

Safe To Spend helps you understand how much money is available after your budget commitments are considered.

Tap the Safe To Spend card on the Dashboard to view the calculation.

The breakdown may include:

  • Available balance
  • Future income
  • Future expenses
  • Recurring items included
  • Safety buffer
  • Available to spend

This view explains how SiBalance arrives at your Safe To Spend amount.

Income

Open the Income tab to view and manage income entries.

From this screen you can:

  • View total income for the current period
  • Add new income entries
  • Search income records
  • Sort income entries
  • Review recurring income

Tap the + button to add new income.

Expenses

Open the Expenses tab to view and manage expenses.

From this screen you can:

  • View total expenses for the current period
  • Add new expense entries
  • Search expense records
  • Sort expense entries
  • Review recurring expenses

Tap the + button to add a new expense.

Insights

The Insights section helps you understand your budget more clearly.

Use the period selector to review insights for different budget periods.

Briefing

A narrative summary of the selected period, including what changed and why it matters.

Forecast

Projects where the current period may finish and highlights unusual movement before it becomes a surprise.

Patterns

Separates recurring costs, one-off activity, and trend behaviour so patterns are easier to see.

Breakdown

Provides structured analysis of income and expenses across categories.

Reports

Use Reports to generate a detailed summary for a selected date range.

To create a report:

  1. Select a start date
  2. Select an end date
  3. Choose which income to include
  4. Choose which expense categories to include
  5. Select the report sections you want
  6. Tap Generate Report

Report sections can include:

  • Summary
  • Changes
  • Recurring & One-offs
  • Smart Notes

Settings

Open Settings from the gear icon on the Dashboard.

Budgets

Create, switch between, and manage separate money plans.

  • View the current budget
  • Add another budget
  • Manage separate income and expenses for each budget

Timeframes

Configure how your budget periods work.

  • Week starts
  • Budget interval
  • Budget starts
  • Date format

Balance

Adjust how balances behave and appear.

  • Carry unused balance: add remaining balance to the next period
  • Always hide balance: keep totals hidden until you reveal them
  • Safety buffer: set money aside from Safe To Spend without changing your balance

Notifications

Choose which budget reminders you want.

  • Daily reminder
  • Payments due
  • Income due
  • Period ending
  • Reminder time
  • Due reminder timing

Appearance

Change the app's look and theme.

  • Light mode
  • Dark mode
  • System mode
  • Theme
  • App icon

Accessibility

SiBalance works with Apple accessibility features to help make budgeting comfortable for everyone.

Accessibility support includes:

  • Dynamic text sizing
  • VoiceOver support
  • Increased contrast support
  • Reduce Motion support

Reduce Motion

If you prefer fewer animations and motion effects, enable Reduce Motion in iOS Settings. SiBalance respects your device accessibility preferences and reduces interface motion where possible.

To enable Reduce Motion:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap Accessibility
  3. Tap Motion
  4. Turn on Reduce Motion

Changes take effect automatically in SiBalance.

Security & Backup

Protect the app and keep data in sync.

  • Device Passcode / Face ID: require device authentication when opening SiBalance
  • iCloud Sync: keep budgets, transactions, categories, and settings available across Apple devices

Use Help and Support for guides and assistance.

  • User Guide: open the SiBalance help guide in your browser
  • Feedback & Bug Reports: send feedback or report a problem by email

Subscription

Manage your SiBalance Premium plan through Apple subscription settings.

  • View your subscription
  • Change your plan
  • Cancel Premium

Data

Use Data settings for permanent actions.

Delete Data removes all saved budget data from this app. This action cannot be undone.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is optional.

When enabled, SiBalance keeps your budgets, transactions, categories, and settings available across your Apple devices using your Apple account.

When disabled, your data remains stored locally on your device.

Managing Your Subscription

SiBalance Premium subscriptions are managed through Apple.

To manage or cancel your subscription:

  1. Open SiBalance
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Manage Subscription
  4. Use Apple's subscription settings to view, change, or cancel your plan

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your saved SiBalance data from the Data section in Settings.

This permanently removes saved budget data from the app.

Warning: deleting data cannot be undone.
