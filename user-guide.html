SiBalance User Guide

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to SiBalance. This guide explains how to set up your budget, manage income and expenses, understand Safe To Spend, and use the app's reporting and insights features.

Getting Started

Create Your First Budget

When you first open SiBalance, tap New Budget.

You can begin a free trial of SiBalance Premium or restore an existing purchase.

Choose Your Currency

  • Browse the supported currencies
  • Search by currency code or name
  • Tap Continue to confirm your selection

Optional iCloud Sync

You can choose whether to enable iCloud Sync.

When enabled, budgets, transactions, categories, and settings are synchronised across your Apple devices using your personal iCloud account.

Name Your Budget

Give your budget a descriptive name such as:

  • Monthly Budget
  • Family Budget
  • Personal Budget
  • Household Budget

Select a Budget Period

Choose how your budget is organised:

  • Weekly
  • Fortnightly
  • Monthly
  • Yearly

You can also choose the day your budget period starts.

Income

Income entries represent money coming into your budget.

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter an amount
  3. Add an optional source name
  4. Choose a date
  5. Enable Repeat for recurring income

Examples include salary, pensions, benefits, freelance income, and other payments.

Expenses

Expense entries represent money leaving your budget.

  1. Select a category
  2. Enter an amount
  3. Add an optional description
  4. Choose a date
  5. Enable Repeat for recurring expenses

Examples include rent, utilities, subscriptions, insurance, and transport.

Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a summary of your current budget.

  • Current balance
  • Total income
  • Total expenses
  • Safe To Spend amount

Use the navigation arrows to move between budget periods.

Hide Balances

Tap the eye icon to hide or reveal balances.

Safe To Spend

Safe To Spend helps you understand how much money is available after future commitments are considered.

Tap the Safe To Spend card to view a detailed breakdown.

Depending on your budget, the calculation may include:

  • Available balance
  • Future income
  • Future expenses
  • Recurring transactions
  • Safety buffer

Insights

Insights help you understand what is happening within your budget.

Briefing

A summary of key changes and activity.

Forecast

Projects where the current period may finish.

Patterns

Highlights recurring and one-off activity.

Breakdown

Provides category-level analysis.

Reports

Generate detailed reports for any date range.

  1. Select a start date
  2. Select an end date
  3. Choose categories
  4. Select report sections
  5. Tap Generate Report

Settings

Budgets

Create and manage multiple budgets.

Timeframes

Configure budget intervals, start dates, and week starts.

Balance

  • Carry unused balance
  • Always hide balance
  • Safety buffer

Notifications

Configure reminders and notification timing.

Appearance

Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance modes.

Accessibility

  • Dynamic text sizing
  • VoiceOver support
  • Increased contrast support
  • Reduce Motion support

Reduce Motion

  1. Open the Settings app
  2. Tap Accessibility
  3. Tap Motion
  4. Enable Reduce Motion

SiBalance automatically respects this setting.

Security & Backup

  • Face ID / Device Passcode protection
  • Optional iCloud Sync
