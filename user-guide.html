Common Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. SiBalance does not require user accounts.

You can create budgets and use the app without registering, signing in, or providing personal information.

Does SiBalance connect to my bank?

No. SiBalance does not connect to bank accounts, financial institutions, or third-party financial services.

All income and expenses are entered manually by you.

Where is my data stored?

Your budgeting data is stored locally on your device.

If you enable iCloud Sync, your budgets, transactions, categories, and settings can be securely synchronised across your Apple devices using your Apple ID.

Is iCloud Sync required?

No. iCloud Sync is completely optional.

If disabled, your data remains stored only on your device.

How do I restore my subscription?

  1. Open SiBalance.
  2. Navigate to the Premium screen.
  3. Tap Restore Purchases.

Any active subscription associated with your Apple ID will be restored automatically.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly through Apple.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap your Apple ID at the top of the screen.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select SiBalance.
  5. Choose Cancel Subscription.

You will continue to have access until the end of the current billing period.

Does SiBalance use ads or analytics?

No. SiBalance does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Third-party analytics
  • Marketing SDKs
  • Tracking technologies
  • Data brokers

Your budgeting information remains private.

Can I use multiple budgets?

Yes. SiBalance Premium supports multiple budgets, allowing you to manage separate financial plans independently.

Examples include:

  • Personal budget
  • Household budget
  • Business budget
  • Holiday budget

Does SiBalance support Face ID?

Yes. You can enable Face ID or device passcode protection from:

Settings → Security & Backup

How do I delete my data?

Open:

Settings → Data → Delete Data

Deleting data permanently removes all budget information stored within the app.

This action cannot be undone.

How do I enable Reduce Motion?

SiBalance supports Apple's accessibility settings.

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Accessibility.
  3. Tap Motion.
  4. Turn on Reduce Motion.

SiBalance automatically respects this setting.
