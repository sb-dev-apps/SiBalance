Privacy Policy for SiBalance iOS

Last updated: June 2026

SiBalance respects your privacy and is designed to help you manage your finances without sacrificing control of your personal data.

Information Collection

SiBalance does not require user accounts and does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal financial information.

SiBalance does not use:

  • Advertising services
  • Third-party analytics platforms
  • Tracking technologies
  • Marketing SDKs
  • Bank account connections

Local Data Storage

All budgets, transactions, categories, settings, and financial information entered into SiBalance are stored locally on your device.

Optional iCloud Sync

SiBalance offers optional synchronization using Apple's iCloud services. When enabled, your data is synchronized through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit technology and private iCloud database.

The developers of SiBalance do not have access to your synchronized data.

App Store Purchase

SiBalance is sold as a paid-upfront app through Apple's App Store.

Payment processing and purchase management are handled securely by Apple. The developers of SiBalance do not receive, process, or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

SiBalance does not include third-party advertising networks, third-party analytics services, user tracking services, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

Data Security

Because your information is stored locally on your device and optionally synchronized through Apple's iCloud services, you maintain control over your data.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

SiBalance is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

support@sibalance.app
